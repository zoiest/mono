* Map of Content (MOC)

** Core Theoretical Modules (Financial Economics I)

1. [[id:2957cc4b-1bfb-45d8-9b10-0a99d51ed808][Deterministic Interest Rates and Valuation]]
   - The discrete interest model, compounding mechanisms, cash flows $B(\Delta), \dots, B(T\Delta)$.
   - Theorem 2.1: Arbitrage-free valuation of deterministic cash flows.
   - Proof of uniqueness of present value through arbitrage strategies.
   - Forward agreements, forward prices, and the Law of One Price.

2. [[id:3d4b788f-0237-4357-9cef-ba77a62e7a03][Stochastic Interest Rates and the Term Structure]]
   - The spot rate matrix and zero-coupon bond pricing $P(0, j\Delta) = (1+s_j)^{-j\Delta}$.
   - Forward interest rates $(1+f_j)^\Delta = (1+s_j)^{j\Delta}/(1+s_{j-1})^{(j-1)\Delta}$.
   - Non-negativity of forward rates and absence of arbitrage: $(1+s_k)^k \ge (1+s_j)^j$ for $k \ge j$.
   - Coupon bonds, yield to maturity (YTM), and interest rate dynamics.

3. [[id:a19aab30-16de-404d-a677-f98770bb0d8d][Duration, Convexity, and Immunization Theory]]
   - Cash flow sensitivity to interest rate perturbations.
   - Macaulay duration and modified duration as first-order measures.
   - Convexity as second-order curvature measure.
   - Redington Immunization ($V_A = V_L$, $D_A = D_L$, $C_A > C_L$).
   - Fisher-Weil immunization and asset-liability management (ALM).

4. [[id:d7075976-269a-4fc0-86b2-27bfcad327f0][Pricing of Financial Securities and Contingent Claims]]
   - State-contingent securities, payoff matrices, and market setups.
   - Derivative instruments: forward contracts, European calls and puts, currency options.
   - Profit requirements, subjective discounting, and market price formation.

5. [[id:32a6be50-9383-466a-89d7-baf20a6ebcb5][One-Period Arbitrage Pricing and FTAP 1]]
   - Formal mathematical framework: assets $S(0) \in \mathbb{R}^N$, payoffs $S(1) \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times N}$.
   - Arbitrage opportunity (Definition 5.1): $S(0)\theta \le 0$, $S(1)\theta \ge 0$, with strict inequality.
   - Pricing kernels / State space deflators $\varphi > 0$ a.s. such that $S(0) = \mathbb{E}[\varphi S(1)]$.
   - The First Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing (Theorem 5.3).
   - Complete proof using the Hahn-Banach Separation Theorem and Corollary 5.5 (Farkas' Lemma).
   - Relationship to Risk-Neutral / Equivalent Martingale Measures $\mathbb{Q}$.

6. [[id:7f47326f-52b0-44c2-8aa8-34845ff9fa7a][Market Completeness and the Second Fundamental Theorem (FTAP 2)]]
   - Attainable contingent claims and replicating portfolios.
   - Complete markets (Definition 5.7): every random variable $X \in \mathbb{R}^M$ is attainable.
   - The Second Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing (Theorem 5.8): Completeness $\iff$ Uniqueness of the deflator $\varphi$ (or martingale measure $\mathbb{Q}$).
   - Dimension criteria: $\operatorname{rank}(S(1)) = M \le N$.
   - Pricing in incomplete markets: intervals of arbitrage-free prices.

7. [[id:365f62e6-88d7-40c6-94f6-6cf0779e7ebf][Portfolio Selection and Expected Utility Theory]]
   - Investor preferences, von Neumann-Morgenstern utility functions $u(z)$, risk aversion ($u'' < 0$).
   - The portfolio optimization problem: $\max_\theta \mathbb{E}[u(S(1)\theta)]$ subject to $S(0)\theta = V(0)$.
   - First-order conditions, Lagrange multipliers, and the endogenous pricing deflator $\varphi \propto u'(V(1))$.
   - Dynamic portfolio selection in multi-period models via Bellman dynamic programming.

8. [[id:9c2d3a35-5605-4e21-901d-6d3e9c82756b][Mean-Square Error Hedging in Incomplete Markets]]
   - Hedging unreplicable claims $X \notin \operatorname{Im}(S(1))$.
   - Quadratic loss formulation: $\min_\theta \mathbb{E}[(X - S(1)\theta)^2]$ subject to budget constraints.
   - Orthogonal projection in $L^2(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$, optimal hedging portfolios, and residual risk.

9. [[id:1dc8f1b2-1399-494c-8ef1-679099444863][The Multi-Period Market Model and Martingale Pricing]]
   - Filtration $(\mathcal{F}_t)_{t=0}^T$, adapted price processes, predictable trading strategies $\theta(t)$.
   - Self-financing condition: $S(t)\theta(t+1) = S(t)\theta(t)$.
   - Discounted asset prices $S^*(t) = S(t)/S_1(t)$ and wealth dynamics $\Delta V^*(t) = \theta(t) \cdot \Delta S^*(t)$.
   - Multi-period FTAP 1 (Theorem 5.10): Absence of arbitrage $\iff$ Existence of an equivalent martingale measure $\mathbb{Q} \sim \mathbb{P}$.
   - Multi-period FTAP 2 (Theorem 5.14): Completeness $\iff$ Uniqueness of $\mathbb{Q}$.
   - The Cox-Ross-Rubinstein (CRR) binomial tree model.

10. [[id:9dd04db2-dde4-4cec-a799-1e68538fd858][Local Risk Minimization in Incomplete Multi-Period Markets]]
    - Non-self-financing strategies and the cumulative cost process $C(t)$.
    - Mean self-financing condition: $C(t)$ is a $(\mathbb{P}, \mathbb{F})$-martingale.
    - Local risk process $R_\theta(k) = \mathbb{E}[(\Delta C(k+1))^2 \mid \mathcal{F}_k]$.
    - Theorem 5.17: Characterization of locally risk-minimizing strategies via orthogonality:
      $$ \mathbb{E}[\Delta C(k+1) \mid \mathcal{F}_k] = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \operatorname{Cov}(\Delta C(k+1), \Delta S(k+1) \mid \mathcal{F}_k) = 0 $$
    - The Föllmer-Schweizer decomposition and minimal martingale measure.

* Fundamental Theorems at a Glance

#+begin_quote
*The First Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing (FTAP 1):*
A discrete-time market model is arbitrage-free if and only if there exists at least one equivalent martingale measure (risk-neutral probability measure) $\mathbb{Q} \sim \mathbb{P}$ under which all discounted asset price processes are martingales.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
*The Second Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing (FTAP 2):*
An arbitrage-free discrete-time market model is complete (every contingent claim can be replicated by an admissible self-financing strategy) if and only if the equivalent martingale measure $\mathbb{Q}$ is unique.
#+end_quote
