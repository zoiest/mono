* Core Syllabus Breakdown

** 1. Fourier Series on the Circle and Torus
- Periodic functions $f(x+2\pi) = f(x)$, Fourier coefficients $\hat{f}(n) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\pi}^\pi f(x) e^{-i n x} dx$.
- Orthogonality of complex exponentials $\{e^{i n x}\}_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ in \(L^2([-\pi, \pi])\).
- Partial sum operators $S_N(f)(x) = \sum_{n=-N}^N \hat{f}(n) e^{i n x} = (f * D_N)(x)$.

** 2. Summation Kernels and Convergence
- *The Dirichlet Kernel* $D_N(x) = \frac{\sin((N + 1/2)x)}{\sin(x/2)}$: non-positivity of $D_N$ and the Du Bois-Reymond failure of pointwise convergence.
- *Good Kernels and Approximate Identities:* definition and properties.
- *The Fejér Kernel* $F_N(x) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} D_k(x)$ (Cesàro summation): positivity and uniform convergence for continuous functions.
- *The Poisson Kernel* $P_r(\theta)$ (Abel summation) and the Dirichlet problem on the unit disk.
- Riemann-Lebesgue Lemma: $\lim_{\lvert n \rvert \to \infty} \hat{f}(n) = 0$.

** 3. Brief Introduction to the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)
- Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) on $\mathbb{C}^N$: naive $O(N^2)$ matrix-vector multiplication.
- The Cooley-Tukey divide-and-conquer algorithm: factorization into even/odd indices yielding $O(N \log N)$ complexity.
- Bit-reversal permutation and practical applications in digital signal processing.

** 4. The Continuous Fourier Transform on $\mathbb{R}$
- Definition: $\hat{f}(\xi) = \int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x) e^{-i x \xi} \, dx$ for $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
- Basic properties: translation, modulation, dilation/scaling, and Riemann-Lebesgue lemma on $\mathbb{R}$.
- Convolutions: $\widehat{f * g}(\xi) = \hat{f}(\xi) \hat{g}(\xi)$.
- Regularity vs. decay duality: smoothness of $f$ implies decay of $\hat{f}$, and decay of $f$ implies smoothness of $\hat{f}$.

** 5. The Schwartz Space $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ and Inversion Formula
- Definition of rapidly decreasing smooth functions: $\sup_x \lvert x^\alpha \partial^\beta f(x) \rvert < \infty$.
- Invariance of $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ under the Fourier transform: $\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{S}) = \mathcal{S}$.
- Differentiating under the integral sign and polynomial multiplication: $\widehat{f'}(\xi) = i \xi \hat{f}(\xi)$, $\widehat{x f}(\xi) = i \frac{d}{d\xi} \hat{f}(\xi)$.
- Fourier Inversion Theorem on $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$: $f(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^\infty \hat{f}(\xi) e^{i x \xi} \, d\xi$.

** 6. Fourier Transform of \(L^2\)-Functions (Plancherel Theorem)
- Extending the Fourier transform from dense subspace $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ to the Hilbert space \(L^2(\mathbb{R})\).
- *Plancherel's Theorem:* $\lVert \hat{f} \rVert_{L^2} = \sqrt{2\pi} \lVert f \rVert_{L^2}$.
- *Parseval's Identity:* $\langle f, g \rangle_{L^2} = \frac{1}{2\pi} \langle \hat{f}, \hat{g} \rangle_{L^2}$.
- Unitarity of the normalized Fourier operator $\mathcal{F}: L^2(\mathbb{R}) \to L^2(\mathbb{R})$.

** 7. Applications to PDEs and Analysis
- *The Heat Equation:* $\partial_t u = \partial_{xx} u$ with initial condition $u(x, 0) = f(x)$. Solving via Fourier transform leads directly to convolution with the Gauss-Weierstrass kernel $K_t(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi t}} e^{-x^2 / (4t)}$.
- *The Wave Equation:* D'Alembert's formula and dispersive properties.
- *The Radon Transform:* Inversion via the Fourier slice theorem (foundational in medical CT imaging).
