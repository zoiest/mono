** Course Worksheets & Weekly Guides

- [[id:384bff66-f65e-4144-b64e-8a200dd58044][Week 01: Foundations of Groups, Permutations, and Finite Symmetries]]
  - Comprehensive lecture notes for Week 01 covering the algebraic hierarchy (Magma $\to$ Semigroup $\to$ Monoid $\to$ Group), homomorphisms, symmetric groups $S_n$, cyclic groups $\mathbb{Z}_n$, roots of unity, small finite groups, Cayley tables, and dihedral groups $D_n$ (§2.1, §2.3, §2.4).
- [[id:ac781a59-5875-4809-ab88-ce4e8a047809][Week 02: Subgroups, Lie Groups, and Young Diagrams]]
  - Comprehensive lecture notes for Week 02 covering subgroup criteria, permutation cycles, alternating group $A_n$, permutation matrices, Young diagrams and integer partitions, Cayley's embedding theorem, classical matrix Lie groups ($\mathrm{GL}, \mathrm{SL}, \mathrm{O}, \mathrm{SO}, \mathrm{U}, \mathrm{SU}, \mathrm{Sp}$), and group actions (§2.2, §2.4, §2.5, §2.8).
- [[id:1f68893e-e151-4c70-a79e-b0f3104e3696][Week 03: Group Actions, Orbits, Cosets, and Quotients]]
  - Comprehensive lecture notes for Week 03 covering equivalence relations, quotient spaces, group actions (translation, conjugation), orbits, stabilizers, Lagrange's theorem, Orbit-Stabilizer theorem, Burnside's lemma, normal subgroups, group centers, and the First Isomorphism Theorem (§2.2, §2.3, §2.9).
- [[id:dfd66ae0-7e03-4e2b-b527-55a902f24962][Week 04: Group Representations, Presentations, and Vector Spaces]]
  - Comprehensive lecture notes for Week 04 covering free groups, presentations $\langle X \mid R \rangle$, Artin braid groups $B_n$, complex vector spaces, linear representations, unitary operators, quantum symmetries, and direct sums / tensor products (§2.7, §3.1, §3.1.1, §3.6.2).

** Chapter 2: Group Theory

1. [[id:5b295c08-7bb4-4d5d-b6be-6fa096201e4d][Groups and Elementary Properties]]
   - Group axioms (G0–G3), Abelian groups (AG4), and the algebraic hierarchy (Magma $\to$ Semigroup $\to$ Monoid $\to$ Group).
   - Uniqueness of identity and inverses, cancellation laws, order of groups and elements.
   - Cayley tables, the Rearrangement Theorem (Latin square property).
   - Abstract groups vs. concrete realizations, external direct products, examples of groups and non-groups.

2. [[id:2b2d79fe-9b2c-4c80-896f-8d79a55ac3c0][Subgroups, Cosets, and Lagrange's Theorem]]
   - One-step and finite subgroup criteria, trivial vs. proper subgroups.
   - The center of a group $Z(G)$, its abelian and normal nature.
   - Left and right cosets as equivalence classes partitioning $G$.
   - Lagrange's Theorem ($|G| = [G:H] \cdot |H|$) and fundamental corollaries (element order, prime order groups).

3. [[id:a9605612-c8a4-42ff-8115-a96f70083b3e][Homomorphisms, Isomorphisms, and Quotient Groups]]
   - Group homomorphisms, preservation of identity and inverses, isomorphisms, and automorphisms.
   - Kernels and images, injectivity criterion ($\mathrm{Ker}(\phi) = \{e\}$).
   - Normal subgroups ($N \triangleleft G$), simple and semisimple groups.
   - Quotient groups ($G/N$), well-defined coset multiplication.
   - The First Isomorphism Theorem ($G/\mathrm{Ker}(\phi) \cong \mathrm{Im}(\phi)$).

4. [[id:206fb1b4-ba32-4675-921a-1b0864eae8e8][The Smallest Finite Groups]]
   - Exhaustive classification of all groups of order $1 \le N \le 8$.
   - Cyclic groups $\mathbb{Z}_N$ and root-of-unity realizations.
   - The Klein four-group $V_4 \cong \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$.
   - The smallest non-Abelian group $S_3 \cong D_3$.
   - Order 8 groups: $\mathbb{Z}_8$, $\mathbb{Z}_4 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$, $\mathbb{Z}_2^3$, the Dihedral group $D_4$, and the Quaternion group $Q_8$ (Hamiltonian group).
   - Complete Cayley tables for all non-trivial small groups.

5. [[id:e61da419-8849-4086-bc86-0892d092c8f4][Permutations, the Symmetric Group, and Cayley's Theorem]]
   - Permutation groups $\mathrm{Perm}(X)$ and symmetric groups $S_n$.
   - Two-line Cauchy notation and disjoint cycle decomposition.
   - Transpositions, parity, signature homomorphism, and the Alternating group $A_n \triangleleft S_n$.
   - Conjugacy classes in $S_n$ classified by cycle structures.
   - Cayley's Theorem: every finite group embeds into a symmetric group.

6. [[id:9231c23d-0041-4dcc-9c4b-8684eeae1b76][Partitions, Young Diagrams, and Multisets]]
   - Integer partitions $N = \sum n_i$, Euler's generating function, and Hardy-Ramanujan asymptotics.
   - Graphical construction of Young diagrams, correspondence with $S_N$ conjugacy classes and irreps.
   - Multisets, multinomial coefficients, the Multinomial Theorem, and anagram combinatorics.

7. [[id:311c6a8f-4c6b-49aa-8d55-ce17aae8415f][Free Groups, Presentations of Groups, and Braid Groups]]
   - Free groups $F(X)$, alphabet, reduced words, and reduction algorithm.
   - Group presentations $G = \langle X \mid R \rangle$ (cyclic, dihedral $D_n$, Pauli group $G_1$).
   - Artin Braid Groups $B_n$: geometric strands, Artin relations, Reidemeister move III / Yang-Baxter equation.
   - Physical applications: Anyons in $(2+1)$D spacetime and topological quantum computing.

8. [[id:16fe12b7-9348-4965-a3b7-73ec71993c39][Continuous Groups and Lie Groups]]
   - Continuous groups, coordinates, group manifolds, and Lie groups ($C^\infty$ operations).
   - Classical Matrix Lie Groups: $\mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{R})$, $\mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{C})$, $\mathrm{SL}(n, \mathbb{R})$, $\mathrm{O}(n)$, $\mathrm{SO}(n)$, $\mathrm{U}(n)$, $\mathrm{SU}(n)$, and $\mathrm{Sp}(2n, \mathbb{R})$.
   - Dimension derivations, compactness, and topological connectedness.
   - Deep dive into $\mathrm{SU}(2)$: topology as $S^3$, Pauli algebra, completeness relation, and exponential map.
   - Octonions ($\mathbb{O}$) and the five Exceptional Lie Groups: $G_2, F_4, E_6, E_7, E_8$.

9. [[id:0c71e7cb-1a90-4ce5-bc72-38c37afda295][Groups Acting on a Set]]
   - Left and right $G$-actions, $G$-spaces, and intertwining equivalences.
   - Orbits, transitive, faithful, and free actions.
   - Stabilizers (isotropy groups / little groups), fixed-point sets, and conjugacy along orbits.
   - The Orbit-Stabilizer Theorem ($\mathcal{O}_x \cong G/G_x$) and homogeneous spaces (e.g. $S^2 \cong \mathrm{SO}(3)/\mathrm{SO}(2)$).
   - Burnside's Lemma (Cauchy-Frobenius) and combinatorial symmetry counting.
   - Group translations, conjugation, centralizers, and complete catalog of group centers.
   - The double cover homomorphism $\mathrm{SU}(2) \to \mathrm{SO}(3)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(2)/\mathbb{Z}_2 \cong \mathrm{SO}(3)$.

10. [[id:2bf3d940-6290-4682-841f-acf960308d2e][Chapter 2 Selected Problems and Worked Solutions]]
    - Problem 2.1: Permutation matrix representation of $S_3$.
    - Problem 2.2: Rectangle symmetries and the Klein four-group $V_4$.
    - Problem 2.3: Invariance groups of $S^2$ and $S^2$ with a marked point ($O(3)$ and $O(2)$).
    - Problem 2.5: Non-Abelian nature of $O(2)$.
    - Problem 2.14: Cycle multiplication in $S_6$.
    - Problem 2.15: Presentation $\langle a, b \mid aba^{-1}b^{-1} \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$.

** Chapter 3: Representation Theory of Groups (Section 3.1)

11. [[id:ef2abac8-273c-404c-8140-d048794366d9][Complex Vector Spaces and Representations]]
    - Complex vector spaces (axioms VS1–VS5), linear independence, dimension, and bases.
    - Linear operators, Rank-Nullity Theorem, $\mathrm{Aut}(V) \cong \mathrm{GL}(n, \mathbb{C})$.
    - Linear group representations $D : G \to \mathrm{Aut}(V)$, dimension, and faithfulness.
    - Intertwining operators and equivalence of representations (similarity transformations).
    - Hermitian scalar products, Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization procedure.
    - Adjoint operators $A^\dagger$, unitary operators $\mathrm{Unit}(V) \cong \mathrm{U}(n)$, and unitary representations.

12. [[id:9548a93b-2f08-4eaa-8c7d-d4a757aed1df][Symmetry Transformations in Quantum Mechanics]]
    - Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ and ray representation of physical states.
    - Schrödinger equation and time evolution generator $H$.
    - Unitary representations of symmetries, invariance of dynamics $\implies [H, U_g] = 0$.
    - Energy eigenspaces $\mathcal{H}_n$ as carrier spaces of symmetry representations.
    - Symmetry-enforced degeneracy vs. accidental degeneracy and hidden dynamical symmetries.
