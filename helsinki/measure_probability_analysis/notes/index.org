* How to Use These Notes

These notes rewrite every chapter of the course lecture notes (/MAST31051: Measure, probability, and real analysis/, T. Kuusi, 2026) for a reader who has *not* taken measure theory.
- Numbering of definitions, theorems and sections follows the original lecture notes, so you can cross-reference.
- Every section starts with intuition, then gives the precise statement, a readable proof (or a clearly marked sketch), and extra worked examples and non-examples.
- Each chapter ends with worked exercises (full solutions) and a summary cheat-sheet.
- Chapter 0 is an extra primer (sets, countability, sup/inf, \([0,\infty]\) arithmetic, topology of \(\mathbb{R}\)). Read it first if any of those feel rusty.

*Suggested path:* 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 (foundations), then 6 → 7 (integration), 8 → 9 → 10 → 11 (approximation and products), 12 → 16 (probability).
